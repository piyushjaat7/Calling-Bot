# Project Goals

The objective is to build a production-grade AI communication platform capable of handling natural voice conversations through a scalable, modular, and extensible architecture.

The system should remain provider-independent, allowing AI models, speech providers, telephony services, and external tools to be replaced without requiring major architectural changes.
